About the business

What we do. We book cargo space with airlines, handle documents, customs and airport transfers, and deliver to the consignee's door.

Target customers. Pharma and electronics exporters, fashion and e-commerce brands, and companies with urgent spare parts.

Service area. Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Bengaluru and Ahmedabad airports, with lanes to Dubai, Singapore, Frankfurt and London.

Key value proposition. Fixed upfront pricing, fast customs clearance and one contact person for every shipment.

Our handling follows safety and cargo rules from DGCA, India's aviation authority. We work with our airport partner AAI Cargo for domestic cargo terminals.
