# SkyHaul Air Cargo: Air Cargo Logistics Service

**Business:** Air freight booking service for exporters, pharma and e-commerce firms.
**Problem:** Shippers do not know the cost of an urgent air shipment until after they call for a quote.
**Customer:** Exporters, pharma and electronics companies, and brands that need fast delivery.
**App:** Business website with an instant air freight cost calculator and an operations dashboard.
**Dashboard:** Shipments handled, tonnes flown, on-time %, delayed shipments, customs clearance time, satisfaction, and tonnes per month.

## Sections
Home, About, Services, Get a quote, Track, Dashboard, Contact/Enquiry.

## Interactive features
1. Shipment tracker: enter SKY1001 to SKY1004 to see status, location and ETA.
2. Cost calculator: It compares actual and volume weight, uses the higher as chargeable weight, then adds service level and a 12% fuel surcharge.
